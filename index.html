Economics

Aditya Srivastava

Financial economics · Information economics · Banking and financial stability

I am an economist working at the intersection of financial economics, information economics and microeconomic theory. My research interests centre on financial intermediation, banking and financial stability, with a particular focus on how information frictions, strategic interaction and networks shape coordination in financial markets. My research experience spans theoretical modelling, global games, information economics and empirical analysis of sovereign debt distress.

Research

Regulatory Communication, Investor Networks & Bank Runs

Master's thesis · Institut Polytechnique de Paris / HEC Paris · 2026

Develops a theoretical framework examining how regulatory communication and investor networks shape coordination under information frictions. The project was conducted in collaboration with the Deutsche Bundesbank Research Centre.

Supervisors: Kartik Anand and Jean-Édouard Colliard

Rationally Inattentive Acquisition of Information in Dynamic Global Games

Master's thesis · London School of Economics · 2024

Master's research on information acquisition in dynamic global games.

Supervisor: Olivier Gossner

Sovereign Debt Distress and Early-Warning Systems

Ongoing research · Finance for Development Lab, CEPREMAP

Current work involves econometric analysis of sovereign debt distress using macroeconomic and financial data, and reproducing and extending the Lab's predictive framework. Planned extensions explore machine-learning and Mixture-of-Experts approaches.
